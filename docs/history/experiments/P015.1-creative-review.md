# P015.1 Creative intelligence review (E11 sky video)

Reviewed: the E11 video `experiments/E11/vc-20261126-ddf90e5d/audio/final-1080x1920.mp4`, its Full HD keyframes and contact sheets, `notes/creative-decisions.md`, `plan/creative-plan.json`, `visual/topic-sky.js`, and the creative-direction and Paper Explainer references as they stood after P014.10.

This is a guidance change only. **Better visual quality has not been demonstrated.** It needs a new video and a human review.

## Observed limitations

| Area | Observation in the sky video |
|---|---|
| Hook | The opening is a small child seen from behind and a static question mark in an empty sky. There's no face, no tension and no strong first read. |
| Vertical space | Content sits in horizontal bands. The rooftop shots leave the upper half as flat sky. The violet shot leaves the lower 40% empty, and the prism room's lower half is a plain wall. |
| Explanatory scenes | The waves and violet shots are textbook diagrams (waves, bursts, a colour bar) on flat tinted backgrounds, with no setting. |
| Depth and material | Most shapes are flat single fills. There's little paper thickness, no varied paper stocks and no foreground framing. |
| Lighting | Light never touches the scene. The prism beam doesn't light the room, and the sun doesn't change shadows. Shadows are uniform drop shadows, not a consistent key light. |
| Character | Three identical rooftop appearances: back view, same pose, no expression, no reaction to the narration. |
| Shot variation | Almost all medium-wide frontal shots. No close-ups, no macro and no changes of viewpoint. |
| Motion | Reveals are mostly pop-ins and draw-ons. There's little cause-and-effect motion and almost no transitions that carry an action across the cut. |

## Root causes in the guidance

1. **Minimalism was encouraged.** "Sparse, labelled symbols" (creative direction) and "a sparse foreground", "one clear visual job" (Paper Explainer) were read as "draw few, flat things".
2. **No composition principles.** Nothing addressed focal hierarchy, use of the 9:16 frame, foreground, middle ground and background, value plans or shot-scale variety.
3. **No hook guidance.** Nothing said the first seconds must carry a striking, question-bearing image.
4. **The self-review only covered defects.** It checked overlaps, empty areas and cropping, never appeal, specificity, light, depth, variety or character, so a technically clean but flat video passed.
5. **No art-direction thinking before artwork.** The plan records objects and cues, not thumbnails, light or composition, so the artwork was written straight into code as diagrams.
6. **The style file lacked any paper craft.** It specified plates and parallax but not thickness, paper stocks, light as material, construction details, puppet characters or diorama-style mechanisms.
7. **Repetition was framed only as a callback.** The guidance asked for callbacks but not for the callback to change.

## Reusable improvements made

- **[creative-direction.md](../../../skills/video-creator/references/creative-direction.md):**
  - A four-role pre-artwork pass: storyboard artist, illustrator, art director, motion designer. Results go to the `creative-decisions` note (no schema change).
  - Universal principles independent of style, language and provider: the opening hook; composition and hierarchy; using the vertical frame; layering; placing subjects in a world; intentional negative space; shot-scale and viewpoint variation; callbacks that change; character expression; cause-and-effect motion with anticipation and follow-through; transitions that continue an action; richness versus clutter; and language independence.
  - The self-review is extended with ten art-direction questions, and the problems are ranked before the one correction is spent.
- **[styles/paper-explainer.md](../../../skills/video-creator/references/styles/paper-explainer.md):**
  - "One clear job" is clarified as one focal idea, not a sparse picture.
  - Paper construction and materials: thickness, paper stocks, vellum for light, hand-cut irregularity, construction details.
  - Key light and a value plan, light that touches surfaces, three-to-five-depth stacks, puppet characters built with `art.parts`, mechanisms as physical dioramas, and use of the vertical paper frame.
  - Paper self-review questions, and a check for plate gaps at every camera position (the E11 sunset strip was found only visually).
  - Paper-physics motion.

No scene templates, object counts or topic-specific instructions were added. Renderer, kit, narration, ledger, preflight and QA are unchanged.

## How to evaluate on an unseen topic

1. Produce the next autonomous video, the Telugu one below, on a topic not used before, following the updated references without any human scene input.
2. **Agent evidence:** the `creative-decisions` note must contain per-shot thumbnails, art-direction choices and written answers to all the self-review questions.
3. **Side-by-side human review** against the E11 keyframes, using the ten art-direction questions as a rubric. Score each from 1 to 5 per shot, done by the owner. The agent may not score its own work as approved.
4. Count the observable changes: distinct shot scales used, scenes without a setting, empty-third regions in keyframes, and whether a character changes pose. Report them as evidence, not as quality claims.
5. Only claim an improvement if the human rubric scores improve. If they don't, record which principle failed in practice.

## Remaining limitations

- The improvement is untested: no new artwork or video was produced in P015.1.
- The kit has no dedicated helpers for vellum light, key-light shadows or puppet rigs. Topic modules must build these from the existing primitives (`layered`, `parts`, `castShadow`). Add helpers only if a new video shows the need.
- Preflight still doesn't detect plate gaps or composition problems. Composition stays a human review gate.
- Agent self-review of art is subjective and can't approve artistic quality.
