## 3. Style-specific artistic acceptance criteria (principles, not quotas; P022.1)

- **Paper Explainer:** pieces read as physical cut paper (soft directional shadows, hand-cut edges, folds), and one clear focal subject per shot. Motion feels handled, not tweened, where the tone suits.
- **Tech Explainer:** convincing materials and precise mechanisms. Light directs attention, UI text is legible, and glow is never a substitute for form.
- **Whiteboard:** one continuous board building an argument, varied line weight, lettering fitted beside its drawing, and a meaningful connector.
- **Flat Science:** accurate science, flat depth (crescent shading, stepped glows), a harmonious palette, and transformations that show the process itself.
- **Data Storytelling:** verified, sourced numbers; zero-based or explicitly relabelled scales; direct labels; and emphasis on the value being narrated.
- **Kinetic Typography:** whole words on their speech cues, intact Indic clusters, rhythm in step with speech, and shapes that carry meaning.
- **X-Ray Explainer:** exterior-to-interior reveal, internals drawn accurately, and verified mechanism ratios.
- **Documentary:** authentic, credited media; crops serve the subject; nothing synthetic is passed off as real.
- **3D Explainer:** real geometry and lighting, purposeful camera moves, and physically consistent motion.

**All styles:** factual accuracy with the claims ledger, legible captions at phone size, native-language review, and no premium-quality claim without owner acceptance.

## 4. Autonomous validation protocol

1. **Keyframes first:** for each topic, run `/video-creator` in autonomous mode up to visual production with the keyframe render only (3–4 keyframes). Run the Skill's self-review, then owner review against §3. One correction pass per style.
2. **Short previews second:** for styles whose keyframes pass, a 4–5 s motion excerpt at 540×960 around the key transformation. Owner review.
3. **Full HD only after owner approval**, one style at a time.

The run order is the reverse of maturity (3D, X-Ray, Documentary and Kinetic first), so the least-proven styles surface problems early.
