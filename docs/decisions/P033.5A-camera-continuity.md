# P033.5A: Continuous camera velocity

Status: implementation pending local validation.

The P033.5 canal lock reported near-zero camera speed at intermediate smoothstep keys. Retain segment interpolation for compatibility. Add opt-in shape-preserving cubic Hermite with time-aware harmonic interior slopes and zero endpoint slopes. Compared with linear interpolation, this removes velocity discontinuities; compared with unrestricted Catmull–Rom, coordinate-wise monotonic bounds reduce overshoot. Coordinate reversals may still stop that coordinate; acceleration and scalar speed are not constrained, and obstacle clearance requires scene-specific checks. Target yaw remains externally authored. No dependencies or renderer changes. Validate unequal key intervals, exact key poses, velocity continuity, coordinate bounds, endpoints, repeatability and existing regressions. Reuse the canal lock scene for comparative motion and reconcile camera measurements before artistic acceptance.
