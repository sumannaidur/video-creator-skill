# Reference-quality comparison (E18: WhatsApp message, te-IN, Tech Explainer, full-bleed)

**Benchmark:** the Viral Styles Launchpad previews, as observed in the P018.1 audit. This is the agent's own assessment, not an owner review. Premium quality is not claimed.

## Artistic gate

- **Keyframes v1:**
  - **Strengths:**
    - Four distinct, consistently lit spaces: a desk at night, an isometric city at night, a server hall, and a bedside at dawn.
    - One continuous hero, the locked message capsule.
    - Dark product-studio lighting with glows and light beams.
    - Tick progress that is easy to read.
    - No repeated diagram.
  - **Defects:**
    - The hook was over-zoomed, cropping the phone under the headline.
    - The capsule was too small to read as the hero.
    - One readout collided with an inset.
- **One correction pass (v2):** wider opening framing, a 44%-wide capsule on the phones, and repositioned tags. Preflight then caught a tag pushed outside the safe area by a transition zoom; it was fixed.
- **Decision:** not substantially below the reference in richness, so the preview and Full HD render went ahead.

## Where it meets the reference characteristics

- Full-bleed frames with short word captions that highlight the spoken word, plus a hook headline with a keyword.
- Callouts use narration words.
- Every frame has an environment.
- One hero object carries the story, with clear cause and effect:
  - lock;
  - travel and one grey tick;
  - refusal at the server;
  - waiting;
  - arrival and two grey ticks;
  - unlock and blue ticks.
- The camera travels in every shot, with rack-focus and iris transitions between spaces.

## Where it remains below

- **Finish:** the illustration is still geometric. The racks and buildings are iso blocks, and the phone is flat vector art. The reference has more refined materials and soft volumetric light.
- **No characters:** no human or animal character appears. The reference relies on characterful heroes.
- **Motion:** the capsule moves on straight polylines, and the scanner and keys use simple tweens. There is no secondary motion or squash-and-stretch.
- **Size:** the city's tiny capsules in the finale are hard to read at phone size.
- **Text:** captions are clean, but Telugu wording and phrasing need native review.

## Accuracy

- All narrated facts trace to WhatsApp's own Help Center and Privacy Policy (`research/claims.json`).
- Caveats are recorded per claim:
  - "not even WhatsApp" applies to personal chats;
  - ticks staying grey don't prove a message is unread;
  - forwarded media is the exception to deletion after delivery.
- No WhatsApp logo or trade dress appears; the chat UI is generic.
