# Creative direction: What happens when we send a WhatsApp message? (te-IN, Tech Explainer, full-bleed)

## Reference targets (P018.1 audit)

- Full-bleed, cinematic dark-studio product lighting (3D Explainer, X-Ray references): glass, glowing emissive parts, light beams, and a large hero in every frame.
- Short word captions with a highlighted spoken word, a hook headline with a keyword, and callouts built from narration words.
- No empty frames, no repeated diagram, a camera that is always moving, and clear cause and effect.

## Visual idea

One hero object, the **message capsule** (the green "హాయ్" bubble sealed inside a glass capsule with a padlock), travels the real route.

It moves from your phone, across the internet, through a server that cannot open it, waits if the friend's phone is off, and arrives where only the friend's key opens it. The grey and blue ticks are the scoreboard.

**Brand rule:** no WhatsApp logo or trade dress. The UI is generic chat (green outgoing bubble, grey/blue ticks).

## Spaces (one per story beat; lit consistently by a cool key from the upper left plus warm practical lights)

| Space | Shots | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Desk at night** | send, lock | A phone on a stand, a warm lamp beam, a window with city bokeh, a mug with steam. "హాయ్" types itself, then send glows. The camera pushes into the bubble; a glass capsule and padlock seal it; two keys appear, one stays and one flies to the friend. |
| **City network** (isometric) | travel, finale | Dark blocks with lit windows and glowing roads. The capsule streaks along a fibre route from your phone to a server tower, and one grey tick lights. The finale pulls back to many phones trading locked capsules. |
| **Server hall** | server, wait | Rows of racks with blinking LEDs and a capsule on a lit pedestal. A scanner lens tries to read it and is refused (red ✕, "చదవలేదు"); a route arrow points to the right phone. For the wait, the capsule rests in a glass vault drawer, an inset shows the friend's dark phone, and a counter rises to 30 days. |
| **Bedside at dawn** | delivered, unlock | The friend's phone wakes, and the capsule descends on a light beam: two grey ticks, while an inset shows the server copy dissolving. The friend's key turns, the shackle pops, the glass melts away, the bubble appears, and the ticks turn blue. |

## Guardrails

- Personal text chat only.
- The server is one illustrative building, not WhatsApp's real infrastructure.
- "Not even WhatsApp" is stated for personal chats only.
- Read receipts are shown simply; no claim that grey ticks mean "unread".
- Simplified, not to scale.
