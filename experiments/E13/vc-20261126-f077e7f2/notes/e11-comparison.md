# Creative comparison: E11 (sky, hi-IN) vs E13 (trains, te-IN)

This is the agent's observation, not a quality verdict. Artistic acceptance is a human gate. Both videos passed the same automated technical checks, and those checks say nothing about artistic quality.

Evidence:
- **E11:** `experiments/E11/vc-20261126-ddf90e5d/render/fullhd/keyframes`, `notes/final-frames/contact.png`.
- **E13:** `render/keys-v1/contact.png`, `render/keys-v2/contact-fix.png`, and the final keyframes listed in the delivery report.

| P015.1 criterion | E11 sky video (P014.10) | E13 trains video (P015.3) |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening hook** | A small child seen from behind and a static question mark in an empty sky | A train already swinging round the bend in frame 1. A girl faces the train with an open mouth; a steering wheel is struck out; she points |
| **Use of the 9:16 frame** | Content in one horizontal band; empty upper sky, lower prism-room wall, empty lower 40% in the violet shot | Upper, middle and lower thirds planned in every shot: sky/hills, train/terraces, girl/fence; bogie, wheel, rail/ballast; pegboard, blueprint, bench |
| **Mechanisms in a world** | The waves and violet shots are diagrams on flat tinted backgrounds | Every mechanism is placed in a setting: beside the coach, along the track, a workshop blueprint, a field from above, a storm |
| **Paper construction and light** | Mostly flat single fills, uniform drop shadows | Offset underlayers on most cut shapes, one key light (upper left), a window beam and lamp glow in the workshop, a headlight at dusk. Light still doesn't visibly touch the treads |
| **Shot scale and viewpoint** | Medium-wide frontal shots throughout | Wide, close-up at rail level, along-track, workshop medium, bird's-eye, a callback and a storm push-in. The along-track view is used three times |
| **Character** | Same back view and pose three times, no face | Three-quarter face. Wonder (o-mouth, pointing), then smile, then raising the ice-cream cone in the payoff. The face is small at phone size |
| **Motion that explains** | Mostly pop-ins and draw-ons | The wheel spins and the ground slides. The set drifts and glides back with overshoot. Tapes unroll along the rails. A gust shoves the set into the flange. Rails glow as the train follows the bend |
| **Callback with change** | The rooftop repeats; only the sky colour changes | The hillside returns as a pull-back while the train follows the bend, and again at dusk with lights on and the girl holding the cone |
| **Remaining weaknesses** | (see E11 notes) | A flat 2.5D train with only a small slide; small, schematic profile drawings; a repeated along-track layout; the flange highlight reads as a red bar; small facial features |

**Process difference:**
- **E11:** artwork was coded directly from the plan.
- **E13:** per-shot storyboard, illustrator, art-director and motion notes came first. The self-review also answered the ten art-direction questions, not only defect checks.

**Human review needed:** a side-by-side rubric scored by the owner (P015.1 evaluation plan). Only that can establish whether the visual quality actually improved.
