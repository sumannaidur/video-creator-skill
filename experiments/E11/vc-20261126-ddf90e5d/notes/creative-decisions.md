# Creative decisions: "Why does the sky appear blue?" (hi-IN, Paper Explainer)

Made by the agent (P014.10, autonomous mode). Human review is still required for all of them.

## Story

| Segment | Role | Idea | Shot(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| s1 | hook | A child on a sunny Indian rooftop looks up: the sky is blue, but sunlight looks white. Why? | rooftop |
| s2 | context | White sunlight hides all the rainbow colours; a prism reveals them | prism |
| s3 | mechanism | Tiny air molecules scatter light in every direction; short blue waves scatter far more than long red ones | air, waves |
| s4 | example | Scattered blue reaches the eye from the whole sky (back on the rooftop) | whole-sky |
| s5 | contrast | Violet scatters even more, but sunlight has less violet and eyes favour blue | violet |
| s6 | resolution | At sunset light takes a long path, blue is scattered away and red-orange arrives; the same rooftop turns orange | sunset, dusk |

## Motifs

- **White paper beam:** sunlight, carrying its hidden colours.
- **Blue paper flecks:** scattered blue light. They appear as the burst, the splinters, the sky full of flecks and the flecks peeling off the sunset path.
- **Paper dots:** air molecules. Symbolic, not to scale.
- **Rooftop with the child, water tank, clothesline and tulsi pot:** the viewer's world. It opens the question, returns at «पूरे आसमान से», and closes at dusk.

## Shots, camera and transitions

- **8 shots**, with boundaries from the resolved plan.
- **Camera:** a gentle tilt up on «देखिए», a push in on «प्रिज़्म», a zoom into the air on «अणु», a pull back on «आसमान», a track along the long path on «लंबा», and a settle on «पहुँचते».
- **Transitions:**
  - Tile dissolves: rainbow tiles from the hook into the prism, blue and violet tiles into the violet contrast, and warm tiles into dusk.
  - Printed paper sheets: up into the air, back to the rooftop, and into evening.
  - A straight cut from air to waves, because it is the same idea seen closer.

## Guardrails (from the claim ledger)

- No wavelength numbers and no 1/λ⁴ law; no gases named.
- The molecules are symbolic.
- The violet band width and the eye glow are illustrative and carry no values.
- Red is shown scattering a little, not never.
- Sunset colours are illustrative.

## Self-review and the one creative correction

- **Keyframes v1:** the sunset shot left the upper half of the frame as empty sky, and the dark chip labels were too small for phone size.
  - **Correction:** I reframed the sunset camera onto the Earth, the path and the low sun (zoom 1.25), and raised the chip label size to 36 px.
  - **Follow-up:** the reframe exposed a strip of the backdrop below the exact Earth plate, so I lowered the camera offset (keyframes v2).
- **Preflight run 1:** the sun's glow crossed the top of the stage on frames 0–11, at the opening camera position. I moved the sun 2% lower, and preflight run 2 passed on all 1,839 frames.

## Known weaknesses (left as they are)

- **Prism room:** the lower half is a plain dark wall above the table.
- **Waves:** the blue wave ends at the molecule, and its splinters overlap the wave tip.
- **Violet shot:** the eye appears only for the last third of the segment.
- **Repeated rooftop:** the rooftop art repeats three times on purpose, as a callback, but the child does not change pose.
- **Fonts:** captions and labels use system fonts (licensing review pending).
- **Native review:** Hindi wording, pronunciation, accent and naturalness are unreviewed.
